# Code of conduct

This project is a place to build security software. Everyone who takes part
in it, in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions, follows these
rules. So does the maintainer.

## What is expected

- Write about the work, not about the person. A review says what is wrong
  with the change and why.
- Assume the other person means well and may know something you do not. Ask
  before you conclude.
- Give reasons and evidence. A measurement or a failing test settles more
  than a strong opinion.
- Accept that a decision can go against you. Decisions are recorded with
  their reasons in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/), and a new fact can reopen one.
- Say so when you are wrong, and when you do not know.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or threats, in public or in private.
- Remarks about a person's origin, sex, gender, orientation, religion, age,
  body, health, or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing what is private to someone else, such as an address or the
  contents of a private message.
- Using the project to harm others. Detection content and test data that
  describe attacks belong here; tooling whose purpose is to attack systems
  without their owner's permission does not.

## Security matters

A vulnerability is not reported in a public issue. See
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainer, [@arelove](https://github.com/arelove), in private. If
the matter is on GitHub, the
[report form](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
on the comment or profile also reaches GitHub's staff, who act without the
maintainer.

A report is read and answered within seven days. Who reported it is told to
no one without their permission.

## What the maintainer may do

In order of weight: ask for a comment to be changed, remove or hide it, lock
a thread, and block an account from the project, for a time or for good. The
person concerned is told what was done and why, and may answer.

## Scope

These rules hold in this repository and wherever someone speaks for the
project.
